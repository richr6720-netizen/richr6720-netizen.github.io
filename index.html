Proof of thinking

The main thing is not what I built. It is how I got there.

Anyone can list tools. These six cases show the part that matters: questioning the obvious answer, measuring, and only then building.

01 · Root cause / DevOps

The outage every dashboard called healthy

Scheduled posts froze while every health check stayed green. I stopped trusting the dashboard and asked the job engine directly: zero workers were listening. The cause was leaked 1.4 GB processes plus a start-up race. I fixed both and added a watchdog.

02 · AI evaluation

I killed my own best idea with a benchmark

For Agent D I wanted 1-bit model compression to work. Instead of arguing for it, I built a task bench and ran it: 1 of 8 tasks passed. I dropped the idea and chose the model that scored 14 of 20 at 23 tokens per second.

03 · Growth / analytics

The growth assumption that was wrong

The plan assumed more reach meant more customers. I traced real buyers back to where they came from and showed that they arrive with intent, not from feeds. The company's marketing plan changed that week.

04 · Graphics / performance

A film studio on one 8 GB laptop

I measured every lever instead of buying hardware. The right GPU path rendered 5× faster, a level-of-detail bake cut a frame from 30–50 s to 10.7 s, and SSD streaming cut peak VRAM from 1.77 to 0.43 GB. Result: a 4:52 Unreal Engine documentary and photoreal brand films.

05 · Research

25 documents in, one decision out

The question was which route to use to recover platinum-group metals from spent catalytic converters. I worked through 24 papers and a patent, found that rhodium recovery is what separates the routes, and delivered a 75-page report with one ranked recommendation.

06 · Systems design

A city-scale emergency network, costed from one model

Designed a proposal for an emergency communications network: 22 radio links, 36 sector radios, 60 connected buildings, an operations centre and drones. Every figure in the English and Pashto editions comes from a single cost model, reviewed in independent rounds before delivery.
